Our Story

Elders Empire Unique Enterprise is a growing Nigerian enterprise focused on quality educational training, household and commercial cleaning products, hygiene solutions and catering contract services.

We believe good service should be useful, dependable and delivered with professionalism. Our goal is to serve children, families, schools, businesses and organisations with solutions they can trust.
